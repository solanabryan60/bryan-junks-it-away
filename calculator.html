WHAT CHANGES THE NUMBER

The load is only part of it.

Access & labor

Driveway and curbside pickups are simplest. Garages, inside pickups, stairs, long carries, tight access, and disassembly can require more labor.

What’s in the load

Furniture and appliances handle differently than mixed household junk. Concrete, dirt, tile, roofing, and heavy construction debris need an individual quote.
